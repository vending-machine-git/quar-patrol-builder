Factions

Unofficial fan-made patrol (army list) builder, unit and weapon quick-reference, printable roster sheet, game tracker, battle report generator, and narrative campaign tool for Quar: Clash of Rhyfles by Zombiesmith.

Includes all 12 Clash of Rhyfles factions from the Faction Profiles and Full Rulebook: Coftyrans, Creevin, Crusaders, Fidwog, Western Arnyara / Aber, Principality of Gwynt, Crusaders of Toulmore, Ergydiwr, Rhyflers of Gloam Hyyn, Hoblyn Valley Rangers, Maebwysh, and Tollyn Maeryn.

My Patrols

▸Your patrols are saved only in this browser, on this device.

Clearing your browsing data or using a private window erases them, and they don't follow you to other devices. Save a backup file now and then: it holds your patrols, stories, current game and saved battles. To share one patrol, use Share link or Copy as text.
